Data deletion & privacy requests

Updated September 7, 2026

You can contact Just Dee Dee Music directly to request access, correction, or deletion of information used by Just Dee Dee Inbox.

  1. Email justdeedeemusic@gmail.com with the subject Just Dee Dee Inbox data deletion request.
  2. Include the platform (Facebook, Instagram, email, or Google Voice), your username or contact address, and the approximate conversation date. Explain which information you want removed or corrected.
  3. We will locate the information and may request reasonable verification that you are the person concerned or are authorized to act for them. Never send a password or access token.
  4. We will review the request promptly, remove information that can be deleted, and explain any applicable retention exception. We will respond within applicable legal time limits and tell you when the request is completed.

What a request covers

Depending on the request, we review conversation copies in JDDM’s Discord workspace, related database records, venue/contact links, spreadsheet fields, summaries, and working copies under JDDM’s control. We preserve unrelated venue and customer information. We may keep a minimal restriction record to prevent deleted conversations from being imported again.

Original messages and connected accounts

Deleting a JDDM copy does not delete your Facebook, Instagram, Google, or Discord account, or automatically erase original messages retained by you or those providers. Please use the original service’s controls for those records. Tell us if your request also concerns an original business record held by JDDM so we can review it.

A JDDM account manager can revoke the integration through the relevant connected-app/business-integration settings. Revocation and deletion are separate actions: revoking access alone does not remove previously imported copies.
