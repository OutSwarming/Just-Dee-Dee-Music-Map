Privacy policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · Updated September 7, 2026

This policy explains how Just Dee Dee Music uses information in Just Dee Dee Inbox and its connected booking workflow.

Just Dee Dee Music operates this tool to organize its own booking conversations. Privacy contact: justdeedeemusic@gmail.com. This policy covers our handling of information; Facebook, Instagram, Discord, and Google also have their own policies.

Information we receive

  • Messages and account information: messages sent to or from the connected Just Dee Dee Music Facebook Page and Instagram professional account; names or usernames, account identifiers, conversation and message identifiers, timestamps, and available attachment descriptions or shared links.
  • Booking information: venue names and locations, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, preferred contact methods, notes, requested or confirmed gig dates, and follow-up dates supplied in conversations or entered by the team.
  • Other connected sources: relevant Gmail conversations, Google Voice text/call/voicemail notifications, spreadsheet records, and calendar events used in the booking workflow. Connecting these sources is controlled by the JDDM team; this tool does not obtain access to a customer’s personal inbox.
  • Operational records: delivery and error records, access authorization, message status, venue links, and records of team actions needed to operate and protect the service.

Only information made available by the connected services is imported. Facebook and Instagram attachments generally remain in their original inbox; Discord may show their descriptions or shared links. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

How we use information

  • Read and respond to booking inquiries and ongoing conversations.
  • Keep messages in a single conversation post and show who needs to reply.
  • Match conversations with existing venues, organize contacts, and maintain official follow-up dates.
  • Prepare gig schedules, reminders, worklists, and internal summaries.
  • Investigate delivery problems, prevent duplicate sends, control access, and handle privacy requests.

Automated matching can make mistakes. The team can review or change a venue link. Sending a reply through the Discord reply form requires a team member to submit it. This tool does not grant permission to send unsolicited bulk messages or to bypass a platform’s messaging restrictions.

Where information goes and who can see it

Conversation copies are stored in Discord posts and are visible to people who have access to the relevant JDDM channels. Google Cloud/Firebase stores conversation metadata, message previews and matching evidence, links, statuses, and delivery records. Google Sheets stores venue/contact records and booking dates; Gmail and Calendar retain their original records. Authorized team computers may hold working copies, diagnostic records, and generated summaries.

Venue details entered into the venue directory can appear in the JDDM map/app according to that directory’s sharing settings. Private message bodies are not intentionally published on the public information pages. The team should avoid placing sensitive personal details into a publicly shared venue record.

We use Meta, Discord, Google, and hosting providers to operate these features. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where these providers operate. Provider-controlled copies, security logs, and retention follow their applicable terms and policies. We may disclose information when legally required or when necessary to protect rights, safety, or the service.

JDDM does not sell information obtained through this tool or use message contents to build advertising audiences.

Local AI and automation

The booking workflow can use an AI model running on a JDDM-controlled computer to help summarize selected records and suggest next steps. The current summary process has no hosted-AI fallback. The resulting summaries may be delivered to the JDDM team through Discord and configured text notifications. This local processing does not make the underlying Google, Meta, or Discord services local.

JDDM does not use private messages from this integration to train or fine-tune a general-purpose AI model. Suggestions and matches are assistance for the team, not decisions that guarantee a booking or establish a contract.

Retention and security

We retain information while it is needed for active inquiries, bookings, follow-ups, legitimate business records, troubleshooting, or applicable legal obligations. There is no single automatic expiration period for every booking conversation. Records that are no longer needed should be removed; verified deletion requests are handled as described below. Limited information may need to be retained for legal obligations, disputes, security, or to honor a request not to reimport a conversation.

We use access controls, protected credentials, and encrypted service connections. Team members must limit access to people who need it. No system can guarantee absolute security. Removing a connection stops future authorized access but does not itself erase copies previously imported.

Your choices and data requests

You can ask about, correct, or request deletion of information held by JDDM by emailing justdeedeemusic@gmail.com. Include the platform, your username or contact address, and enough context to find the conversation. We may ask for reasonable verification of your connection to the information. Do not send your password.

We will review requests promptly and respond within applicable legal time limits. If an exception prevents full deletion, we will explain it where permitted. See data-deletion instructions for the process and the distinction between JDDM copies and records retained by Meta or other providers. Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide additional rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, or complaints to a privacy authority.

Children and policy updates

This is a business booking tool and is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child’s information was collected inappropriately. We may update this policy when the workflow changes; the updated date will appear on this page, and additional notice will be provided where required.
