Just Dee Dee Inbox

App terms

Effective September 7, 2026

Just Dee Dee Inbox is operated by Just Dee Dee Music for its team’s booking communications. It connects authorized JDDM accounts and organizing tools. Customers can continue to communicate through their usual messaging service and do not need to create an Inbox account.

Authorized use

Only people authorized by JDDM may operate its internal controls. Use accounts and contact information you are permitted to access. Protect credentials, check the recipient and venue before sending or saving, and respect requests to stop contact. Do not use the tool for spam, deceptive messages, unlawful content, or attempts to bypass Meta or another service’s restrictions.

Messages, records, and availability

Submitting a reply form can send a real message as Just Dee Dee Music. Ordinary Discord chat is not automatically sent to Facebook or Instagram. Delivery can fail or be delayed; check a conversation before retrying an uncertain send. Service availability, message history, and sending windows depend on the connected providers and the access they grant.

Automated matches, summaries, reminders, and suggestions can contain errors and require human review. A suggested date, lead, draft, or imported calendar item is not by itself a confirmed booking. Booking arrangements remain subject to direct confirmation between the parties.

Privacy and third-party services

The privacy policy explains JDDM’s handling of information. The terms and policies of Meta, Google, Discord, and other connected providers also apply to their services. This tool is operated by JDDM and is not endorsed by Meta.

Changes and contact

JDDM may change or suspend features as operational or provider requirements change. Questions, privacy requests, and reports of misuse can be sent to justdeedeemusic@gmail.com.
